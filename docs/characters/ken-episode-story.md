# Ken Episode Story Plan

> Status: Portfolio summary
> Source note: [ken-episode-story.ko.md](ken-episode-story.ko.md)

This document summarizes the long-term story direction for Ken's Vancouver survival route.

## Episode Structure

Each day should be built from a few memorable event blocks rather than many disconnected moments.

Recommended rhythm:

- Morning, afternoon, evening.
- Travel, destination, consequence.
- Preparation, action, fallout.

Each event block can contain around three normal scenario nodes. This creates roughly 9 to 10 main episodes per day, followed by a summary node.

## Why This Structure Works

The player only needs to remember a few big story beats per day, while the game can still apply steady stat pressure through connected choices.

This helps with:

- Readability.
- Pacing.
- Stat balance.
- StoryMap clarity.
- Future Day and Week expansion.

## Planned Day Arc

### Day 1: Arrival And First Crisis

Core beats:

- Airport landing.
- Immigration pressure.
- Lost baggage.
- Meeting a possible helper.
- First meal and first lonely night.

Purpose:

Day 1 should feel like a harsh initiation. Ken is excited, but Vancouver immediately tests his English, stamina, and judgment.

### Day 2: Basic Survival

Core beats:

- Shopping for essentials.
- Payment mistakes.
- Phone or service setup.
- Credit check friction.
- First restaurant and tipping confusion.

Purpose:

Day 2 shifts Ken from visitor to temporary resident. The pressure becomes less dramatic than the airport, but more practical and constant.

### Day 3: Recovery And Reconnection

Core beats:

- Baggage update.
- Walking through the city.
- Small hidden moments of relief.
- Reconnecting with lost resources.

Purpose:

Day 3 should give the player recovery and confidence after the first two survival-heavy days.

### Later Days

Future days can explore:

- Campus administration.
- First class.
- Group work pressure.
- Housing search.
- Rent scams and contracts.
- Social relationships.
- Week 1 summary and reset.

## Special Event Direction

Special events should not feel like random side quests. They should be short pressure spikes or memorable social situations that connect back to Ken's core themes:

- Misreading kindness.
- Being too trusting.
- Overcompensating with confidence.
- Learning practical boundaries.
- Using embarrassment as a learning moment.

## Summary Node Direction

Summary nodes should not repeat the full story. They should:

- Close the emotional arc of the day.
- Mention the player's survival progress.
- Restore or adjust stats when appropriate.
- Prepare the next day's theme.
- Deliver one useful survival insight.

## Portfolio Notes

Ken's route is built as a learning curve. The early game should be funny, uncomfortable, and occasionally painful, but the larger arc should show that repeated mistakes can become cultural fluency and personal resilience.
