# Privacy Policy Draft

This document is the source copy for the public privacy policy page that will be linked in Google Play Console.

## Summary

ReadTheRoom is a story game that stores game progress locally on the device and uses ad-related services for rewarded ad testing and future monetization.

## Data We Collect

- Local game progress and preferences stored on the device.
- Device and ad interaction data handled by the ad SDK provider.
- Contact information only if you choose to email us.

## Data We Do Not Collect

- We do not require an account for gameplay.
- We do not intentionally collect names, passwords, or contacts for gameplay.

## Third-Party Services

- Google Mobile Ads may collect device identifiers, usage data, and diagnostics for ad delivery.
- Expo services may be used during development and build distribution.

## Local Storage

Game progress, tutorial preferences, and language preferences may be stored locally so the app can restore your session.
