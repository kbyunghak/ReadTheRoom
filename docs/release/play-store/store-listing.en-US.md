## App Name

Read The Room

## Short Description

A bilingual story game about reading social situations and choosing what to say next.

## Full Description

Read The Room is a bilingual choice-driven story game set around everyday social situations in Vancouver.

Play through scenes where timing, wording, and confidence matter. Each choice changes the story flow, your stats, and the kind of feedback you receive after each scene.

Features:

- Korean and English text support
- Choice-based story progression
- Character-specific routes
- StoryMap for revisiting completed scenes
- Feedback and stat changes after each choice
- A replayable story structure with multiple endings and branches

This version is focused on the core story experience and may continue to receive content updates, balance tweaks, and new scenes.
